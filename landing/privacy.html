Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

What We Collect

When you purchase a template we collect your email address (via Stripe) to send your download link. When you join our waitlist we collect only your email. We do not collect names, addresses, or payment details — those are handled entirely by Stripe.

How We Use It

  • Send your download link after purchase
  • Notify you when CRM early access opens (waitlist only)
  • Measure aggregate traffic via Meta Pixel (anonymized)

Templates & Local Data

All form data you enter into TradeOpsVault templates stays in your browser. We never receive, store, or transmit the job information you type into your forms.

AI Analysis

When you run an AI Job Review, the form content is sent to our server and forwarded to Anthropic's API to generate a response. We do not log or store the content of AI analyses.

Third Parties

  • Stripe — payment processing. Governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • SendGrid — transactional email delivery.
  • Meta Pixel — aggregate ad performance measurement.
