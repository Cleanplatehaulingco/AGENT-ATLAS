Features

What this app does

Agent Atlas is a purpose-built operator console for managing one Etsy shop. Every feature exists to serve the daily workflow of a single seller.

📋
Listing Management
Draft, edit, and publish listings to the TradeOpsVault Etsy shop. Manages titles, descriptions, tags, pricing, and digital file uploads through the Etsy API.
📊
Revenue Tracking
Reads shop transactions and order data to track revenue against business goals. All data is fetched on demand and stored only in the owner's browser.
🖼
Image Publishing
Uploads generated product mockup images to Etsy listings in the correct slot order — keeping shop presentation consistent without manual uploads.

About

Built for one shop

Built and operated by the
owner of TradeOpsVault

Agent Atlas is not a commercial SaaS product and is not offered to other sellers. It is a personal automation tool built by the TradeOpsVault shop owner to streamline their own daily workflow — creating printable business form templates for trade contractors.

The application runs entirely in the browser with no backend server. OAuth tokens are stored locally and never transmitted to any third party.
